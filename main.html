2011-2012

The Popularisation of SSDs for gaming

In 2011, the prices of SSD memory decreased from costing 120 times as much as HDDs in 2007 to 32 times in 2011 (Perry), becoming more affordable for personal computers. They continued to become more popular in 2012 due to reduced costs and more vendors entering the storage market of SSDs (Ngo). SSDs use NAND flash memory that allows faster read/write speeds and lower latency than HDDs (Intel), thus games launch and load faster (Intel). SSDs moreover reduce the frequency of “hitching” (the brief pauses when games cannot pull assets from the hard drive fast enough to keep up with in game responses) (Wilde), that is especially common in open world games. Thus, the performance of open world games indefinitely benefit from SSDs.

December 2015

Rainbow 6 Siege

Rainbow 6 Siege is Ubisoft’s tactical shooter themed as counter terrorism sieges. Players play in teams of 5 as operators of counter terrorism units to complete certain objectives usually against an opposing team, like planting/diffusing a bomb, rescuing/defending a hostage, etc. Match gameplay starts with 45 seconds of preparation where attackers can scope out the map using camera drones and defenders can destroy said drones and reinforce certain areas of the map. Then, everyone is released and the team that completes their objective or kills the enemy team wins. Players only have one life per round so caution is advised. Teamwork and cooperation is encouraged to make use of every unit’s different abilities to complete the objective.

A unique feature of this tactical shooter is its emphasis on environment destruction. Maps are full of walls, floors, and structures that can be broken using guns, brute force, and explosives to create pathways and openings. Players can creatively use terrain to plan strategies and gain advantage over the opposing team. A downside of this is that it requires in-depth knowledge of maps, resulting in a larger difficulty curve than the average tactical shooter.

The game was praised for its tactical focus and environment destruction mechanics that created open-ended gameplay, keeping each match fresh. It won a few rewards and though initial reception was mid, it made a glorious comeback thanks to post launch support. After its lackluster launch, Ubisoft applied the “games as service” model and continued to fix bugs and provide free content like new maps and units. These improvements were not lost on gamers as post launch player growth exceeded Ubisoft’s expectations, and they have plans to continue support (Mello-Klein, 2017).

February 2016

Stardew Valley

Stardew Valley is an indie, cozy, farm life simulation game by Eric Barone, sporting a charming pixel art style. You play as a guy who moves to a podunk town next to the titular Stardew Valley after inheriting your dead grandpa’s farm. You’re tasked with restoring it and the town to its former glory. Gameplay involves running around tending to and growing your farm, eventually running down your daily energy bar and then repeating the next day. You collect materials from the farm to fulfil each season’s bundle, which you can then take to the town center to unlock new structures and activities. There are 5 main skills: farming, foraging, mining, fishing, and combat. As you interact with the world, you earn XP for the corresponding skill. You can also interact with NPCs to build relationships and eventually marry one of the 12 love interests, which lets them help on your farm.

The game quickly endeared itself to casual gamers, with its relaxing gameplay loop of farming and expanding the town. It is one of the best selling video games ever and earned a ton of awards. The initially Windows only game was ported to many platforms and even received a multiplayer update, allowing online co-op for up to 4 players. This game has also garnered a large modding community, which have created mods that add content, change gameplay, improve aesthetics, and more.

August 2018

NVIDIA RTX (realtime ray tracing)

In August 2018, Nvidia announced its first card in their GeForce graphical processing unit (GPU) series, the GeForce RTX 2080 (Burnes, 2018). It was the first GPU card that provided real time raytracing to consumer products (i.e. video games), technology that was previously only utilised in movie VFX due to computing limitations.

Ray tracing (new): It’s basically working backwards from the viewer’s eye to track the path of individual light rays to the source, mimicking the physics of light in real life (Caulfield, 2018). It’s more accurate than the old method of rasterization (see below) as it can more accurately represent light interference like shadows, reflection, and refraction to create photorealistic renders (Caulfield, 2018). It is also a lot more computationally intensive because raytracing requires a separate process for each pixel as opposed to other methods which could share data between multiple pixels.

Rasterization (old): This uses the vertices of the polygon meshes of 3D assets to map 3D forms onto a 2D screen, then applying shaders to each pixel (Caulfield, 2018). It uses the scene’s geometry to compute the colour of the pixel on screen instead of assigning what colour a pixel should be (Prunier, n.d.). It also pre-renders upcoming frames ahead of time which uses up some memory. Graphics quality can be questionable due to pixel precise colouring but it can be improved by anti-aliasing (Mednafen/Beetle PSX – Pgxp Arrives! – Libretro, 2016).

March 2021

It Takes Two

It Takes Two is Hazelight Studios' co-op action-adventure game, written and directed by Josef Fares (of A Way Out and 'the Oscars are a joke' fame) and published by Electronic Arts in March 2021. The story follows Cody and May, a married couple on the verge of divorce who, after their daughter Rose wishes for them to reconcile, are magically trapped in the bodies of two handmade dolls. The only way to return to human form is to work together and traverse a series of fantastical, storybook worlds - a garden shed, a treehouse, a snow globe, a clock tower - each built around a single paired mechanic like a magnet glove, a nail gun, or time reversal. The game is strictly two-player: there is no single-player mode, no online matchmaking, and every puzzle and combat encounter is designed around two controllers, either couch co-op or online. It Takes Two was a surprise critical and commercial hit, selling over a million copies in its first month and going on to win Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2021, beating out Resident Evil Village and Deathloop (The Game Awards, 2021). It is widely regarded as the definitive co-op game of its generation.

February 2022

Elden Ring

Elden Ring is an open-world action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment, released February 2022. Directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki with worldbuilding and lore written in collaboration with fantasy novelist George R. R. Martin, it takes the punishing, tight melee combat of the Souls series and drops it into a vast, seamless open world called the Lands Between. Players create a custom 'Tarnished' and explore six large regions - each with its own geography, bosses, and dungeons - riding a spectral steed called Torrent and jumping freely between cliffs, ruins, and castles. Unlike earlier Souls titles, Elden Ring offers multiple build paths, spirit summons, and stealth or magic options, softening the genre's notoriously harsh difficulty while keeping its core combat unforgiving. It became FromSoftware's fastest-selling game ever, moving over 12 million copies within a month and 20 million by late 2022, and swept the Game of the Year awards at The Game Awards 2022, the Golden Joysticks, and the Game Developers Choice Awards (The Game Awards, 2022). Its success proved that a brutally difficult, 'git gud' game could also be a mainstream blockbuster.

August 2023

Baldur's Gate 3

Baldur's Gate 3 is a role-playing game developed and published by Larian Studios, the Belgian team behind Divinity: Original Sin 2, released in full in August 2023 after three years in early access. It is based on Dungeons & Dragons' fifth edition ruleset and set in the Forgotten Realms universe. Players create a custom character (or choose one of seven pre-made Origin characters) who has been infected with a mind-flayer tadpole parasite that threatens to turn them into a monster. The story follows the party across the Sword Coast as they search for a cure, allying with a chaotic cast of companions including a vampire spawn, a fiendish warlock, and a githyanki fighter. Combat follows D&D's turn-based action economy with rolling dice for attacks and saving throws, and the game is famous for its staggering freedom - nearly any NPC can be killed, nearly any quest has multiple solutions, and player choices lead to radically different endings, including the option to romance or betray almost every companion. It won Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2023 and is widely regarded as the best CRPG in decades, reviving interest in the genre for a new generation (The Game Awards, 2023).

August 2024

Black Myth: Wukong

Black Myth: Wukong is an action role-playing game developed and published by Chinese studio Game Science, released August 2024. It is loosely based on Journey to the West, the 16th-century Ming dynasty novel by Wu Cheng'en, casting the player as the Destined One, a successor to Sun Wukong (the Monkey King), who journeys across a mythical version of China collecting the Great Sages' relics and confronting gods and demons. Gameplay is a fast-paced third-person character action game with a long staff that can transform into different forms - a massive pillar, a double-bladed staff, a staff that shoots projectiles - alongside equippable spells, transformations, and consumables borrowed from Wukong's own bag of tricks. Boss fights are large, multi-phase affairs against reinterpretations of classic Journey to the West demons, rendered in Unreal Engine 5 with photorealistic Chinese landscapes, temples, and folklore. It became a global phenomenon, selling over 10 million copies within its first month and topping charts worldwide, and was voted Game of the Year at the 2024 Steam Awards (Steam, 2024). It is widely seen as the first Chinese AAA game to achieve breakout Western mainstream success.
